# Nick 360 formulas

These are our definitions. A reference name is the label from the sample workspace. It is not a claim that a third-party formula was copied. Prices on the sample video are illustrative. This page computes from the desk files already published under data/ohlc and the existing CSV tables. It does not download prices, and it does not read or publish the desk's private price cache.

SPY and QQQ daily bars are not in the published per-ticker OHLC files. The regime uses market_today.csv breadth and the stored day moves. It is not a SPY 25 model, a QQQ 33 model, or STEM.

## Measurement choices

- Simple moving averages. Periods 10, 20, 50, 150, and 200. Colors match the stock box: 10 green #22c55e, 20 orange #f97316, 50 blue #3b82f6, 150 yellow #eab308, 200 red #ef4444.
- The stock box entry is amber #F59E0B, the stock box stop is red #EF4444, and the stock box target is green #22C55E. Those three come from scores.csv and are not recalculated. Our 20-session pivot is indigo #a5b4fc.
- A session is one stored daily bar. Windows count bars, not calendar days. Missing sessions are not invented.
- A close that is not a positive number is dropped. A flat bar is kept. Duplicate dates keep the last row. Volume that is not a number is missing, and a window that includes it is blank rather than zero.
- Relative volume is today divided by the mean of the 50 bars before today. Today is not in that mean. A later bar is not used. A zero mean is blank.
- The dry-up ratio is the mean of the last 10 bars, including today, divided by the mean of the 50 bars before those 10.
- Pressure splits a flat close 50/50. It is a price-volume share, not a claim about institutional intent.
- Rank weights are 40% for the latest 63 bars and 20% for each of the three 63-bar stretches before that. Ties take the midpoint percentile, then the result is scaled from 1 to 99. Fewer than two scored names leaves the rank blank. A name with a missing leg is left out of that day.
- The 252-session high uses highs and the low uses lows, including the current bar. Fewer than 252 bars leaves those checks missing.
- Stage uses the 200-day average versus its value 21 bars earlier. A slope inside plus or minus 0.5% is flat. Stage 2 is a close above a rising average.
- Swing pivots used by the tight-base candidate need 4 strictly lower bars on the left and 3 on the right. A tie is not a swing. The swing is only used once the right-hand bars exist, so the last three bars cannot confirm a new pivot.
- Paper statistics, the position calculator, the partial-sale fraction, and the scenario trade count use only numbers the user types. A non-positive edge does not produce a trade count. Mean expectancy compounding is not a forecast. A 2-to-1 payoff does not change the share count. Stops do not cap gaps.
- Quarterly sales are not in the stored files. EPS is shown only where earnings_history.csv has an actual. Headlines are shown only from news.csv.
- IPO and power-IPO screens are unavailable because a listing date is not stored. The first bar in a file is the cache start. Power Play and 3C are not implemented.
